# Math Garden

A second-grade math practice game. One HTML file, no install, no internet needed.

Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Modes

- **Learn**: pictures, hints and "Show me how" explanations. A problem missed on the first try
  comes back a few questions later (up to twice) until it's answered right the first time.
- **Quiz**: 10 questions, one try each, 0 to 3 stars, then a review of the misses.
  "Practice misses" starts a Learn session with exactly those problems.

## Levels

Each topic has 3 to 5 levels. Levels 1 to 3 make the numbers harder. Levels 4 and 5 replay
levels 2 and 3 but make you type the answer on a number pad, so guessing stops working.

- Learn: 5 right in a row (first try) levels up; 3 first-try misses in a row drops a level.
- Quiz: 9 or 10 out of 10 levels up; 3 or fewer drops a level.
- A parent can also pick any level with the − / + buttons before starting.

## Topics

Fast Facts · Adding Big Numbers · Subtracting Big Numbers · Place Value · Skip Counting ·
Times × (bonus) · Sharing ÷ (bonus) · Bigger or Smaller · Even or Odd · Money · Telling Time · Word Problems

Wrong answer choices are built from common mistakes (forgetting to regroup, adding instead of
multiplying, misreading the minute hand), so a wrong pick shows what to practice.

Levels and best scores are saved in the browser on that device only.
